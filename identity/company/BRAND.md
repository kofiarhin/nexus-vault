# Nexus Brand

## Positioning

Nexus is an AI-native productivity and knowledge operating system built around durable user-owned context.

## Brand Principles

- **Clear:** expose how information is organised and retrieved.
- **Durable:** keep knowledge portable in Markdown and Git.
- **Controlled:** separate deterministic operations from AI reasoning.
- **Useful:** optimise for action, planning, and project understanding rather than novelty.
- **Honest:** distinguish known facts, decisions, assumptions, and generated suggestions.

## Voice

Direct, thoughtful, technically credible, and calm. Avoid inflated claims about autonomy or intelligence.
